# Complaint Policy (example, fictional company)

> Example document created for the Hardware Service Decision Copilot PoC. It describes a fictional electronics retailer. It is not legal advice and does not reproduce any real company's terms.

## C1. Complaint period
C1.1. A customer may file a complaint about a product that does not work as intended or has a defect within **24 months** from the date of purchase.
C1.2. A complaint filed after 24 months is not accepted under this policy. The customer may use a paid repair service.

## C2. Covered defects
C2.1. Manufacturing and material defects: dead pixels, backlight bleeding, battery swelling, failing buttons, ports, hinges or speakers, failure to power on, overheating under normal use.
C2.2. Defects that appear during normal use in accordance with the user manual.
C2.3. Cosmetic defects present at delivery (scratches or dents reported within 14 days of purchase).

## C3. Excluded damage
C3.1. Mechanical damage caused by the user: cracked screen or housing from a drop or impact, bent chassis, broken connectors from force.
C3.2. Liquid damage, corrosion, moisture indicators triggered.
C3.3. Damage caused by unauthorised repair, opening the device, or use of non-compatible chargers or accessories.
C3.4. Normal wear and tear: minor scratches and scuffs from everyday use, worn keyboard keys, battery capacity loss within manufacturer specification.

## C4. Decision rules
C4.1. **Approve** when the complaint is within C1, the description and photo indicate a covered defect (C2), and there are no signs of excluded damage (C3).
C4.2. **Reject** when the complaint period (C1) has passed, or the photo clearly shows excluded damage (C3) that explains the reported problem.
C4.3. **Manual review** when the cause of damage cannot be determined from the photo and description, the photo does not show the reported problem, the photo shows a different product, or the description and photo contradict each other.

## C5. Next steps after approval
C5.1. The customer sends the product to the service centre or brings it to a store, with a short description of the problem.
C5.2. The service centre inspects the product. The final decision follows the inspection.
C5.3. The service centre repairs the product first. If repair is impossible or would take longer than 14 days, the product is replaced. If replacement is impossible, the customer receives a refund.

## C6. Next steps after rejection
C6.1. The customer may order a paid repair quote from the service centre.
C6.2. The customer may provide additional information or photos; staff will re-assess the case.
